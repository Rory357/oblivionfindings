Event Horizon hero — the meters direction

Chosen direction (from the variant round): meters — small instruments instead of flat stat pills. Revision: the blocks now live on their own full-width row under the identity/actions row, so every page can dock as many blocks as it needs — and every block is clickable, taking you straight to its view (Hazards → the open-hazards view, Occupancy → beds, Checks → audits…). Hover any block below to see the affordance. Filters stay in the band; the connected-tab rail stays on the bottom edge.

Block library

The five block types every page composes from

One shared anatomy — head (label + headline value), visual, caption — in five flavours. A page picks its blocks and their tones; the row lays them out. Every block is a link to its view (hover: lift + brighter border + corner ↗). Safety tones (critical/warning) are the fixed status colours; neutral blocks take the brand tint.
