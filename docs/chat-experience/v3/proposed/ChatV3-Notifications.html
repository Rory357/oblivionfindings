Why it works this wayC7.7 and C1.4 · built from the plan, decided 22 October

The app’s row, with Chat’s words

Chat rows use the same notification row as every module: who or what, what happened, the newest line, the time and an unread dot. A small mark on the photo says what kind it is: @ for a mention, a red phone for a missed call, a red triangle for urgent.

One row per conversation

Six new messages in one chat are one row that says “6 new messages”, not six rows. The row moves to the top and its time updates. Opening the conversation anywhere clears it: the Chat page, a side sheet or another tab.

What needs you stays visible

A mention stays named in its row (“Ana Rēweti mentioned you · and 2 more messages”). Urgent messages stay until you confirm. A family row keeps its reply promise until someone in the care team replies.
