# Chat v3: proposed boards (awaiting Stephan's approval)

Drawn 31 October 2026 for the five screens that had no v3 board (see `../README.md`). **Not approved yet.** Nothing is built from them until Stephan approves each one.

| Board | Plan item | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `ChatV3-Settings.html` | C7.8, C1.11 | Settings › Chat on the Fleet settings pattern: the overview of review cards (Service health is one), the tabs, a Site's own settings, the save bar and its review, failure and conflict states, and My Chat preferences. |
| `ChatV3-Investigations.html` | M22, C1.12 | The investigator's own page: cases, one case with its read-only conversation view and access log, the finder, the export pop-up and its states, and the watermarked PDF. |
| `ChatV3-Notifications.html` | C7.7, C1.4 | The Chat notification row in the bell and on the Notifications page (one row per conversation), every row kind and state, and browser notifications with their privacy rules. |
| `ChatV3-SideSheet.html` | C7.3 | One conversation in a side sheet over another page, where it opens from, its states and its width and stacking rules. |
| `ChatV3-Popups.html` | C1.0e | Every Chat pop-up on the app's pop-up kit, their common states, and which of today's dialogs each one replaces. |

**How to open:** from your checkout, open a board in a browser. They use the approved boards' stylesheets and logos in `../boards/`. Add `#d` to the address for dark mode.

**Questions for Stephan:** `NOTES-settings-investigations.md` and `NOTES-row-sheet-popups.md` list the scenes, the decisions to check and the open questions for each board.

**Source:** `source/` holds the scripts that drew them (reference only; they read paths on Rory's PC).
