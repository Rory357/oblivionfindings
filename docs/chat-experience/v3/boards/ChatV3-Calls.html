Scope
What calls can do

Voice calls for up to 8 people and video for up to 4, screen sharing, call links for staff huddles, ringing a whole care team, and a call record afterwards.

What they never do

No recordings or transcripts. Family can’t start call links or see who is online. Your camera stays off until you choose to turn it on.

When you can’t be reached

Missed calls show in Calls and in your notifications. Off shift, calls go to the care team instead of you.
