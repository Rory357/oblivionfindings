Why this layoutMeasured on oblivionfindings.com at 1440 × 900, 22 October

Messages get the screen

Today’s family thread gives messages 191 px of a 900 px window (21%). The slim bar and inline tabs give them about 690 px of 900 (77%).

Today · 191 pxProposed · ~690 px
One bar instead of three rows

The privacy banner, the owner row (Reassign, Staff coordination, Hand over) and the “Needs reply” row become one 42 px bar: who can read it, the owner, the reply clock and two actions. Everything else moves to the ⋯ menu and the Details panel.

Familiar from WhatsApp and Teams

Grouped bubbles with one name and photo, reactions under the bubble, photos as a grid, links you can click, typing and read rings. Actions appear on hover and right-click, not in a pop-up.

Conversation list partsRows carry presence, sender, drafts, mutes and mentions; right-click and the row kebab open the same menu
