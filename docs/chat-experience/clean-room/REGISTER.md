## Notices

- `scripts/chat-license-notices.mjs` writes `public/legal/chat-third-party-notices.txt` before every build. Since 22 October it also lists ts-debounce, which is copied inside livekit-client rather than installed. The @stablelib/base64 and @stablelib/utf8 entries went with pusher-js on 23 October.
- Guards against the replaced live-update engines returning: `tests/Unit/Chat/ChatReplacedEnginesGuardTest.php` (Composer and npm manifests) and ESLint `no-restricted-imports` for `pusher-js`, `laravel-echo` and `ws`; the gateway in `live/` may import only `node:` built-ins and its own files.
- `deploy/chat-calling/licenses/` holds the server-side licence copies. Remove each one when its engine is replaced.
- The project is `oblivion/care` with a proprietary licence in `composer.json` (it was the Laravel starter kit's name and MIT licence until 22 October).
