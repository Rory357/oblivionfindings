Use a same-application typed service call. H&S presents canonical source identity/revision to Workforce; Workforce rereads it through the H&S access service. Never accept a client-supplied worker, assessor, approval boolean or clinical narrative as operational authority. No new external transport, collector, tenant key, second eligibility engine or queue.

Self-approval and any override remain pending the already-open modified-duty policy decision. Internal source IDs may bind the service receipt; ordinary roster output must not leak an inaccessible injury ID/link, medical collection count or evidence metadata. ## 4. Save, evaluation and recovery

An approved decision command is one physical-root transaction: governing mutex → current actor/source/worker/Site locks → exact expected source + operational revision → checked normal projection/immutable decision/audit writes → canonical operational fingerprint → existing `WorkforceEligibilityRefresh::stage` once → exact persisted intent/readback and final authority. Register only the approved operational projection in `WorkforceEligibilitySources`; do not also register raw clinical models or double-stage via a second callback. `stage:80` currently uses an unchecked `save`; the new command must verify the exact staged row/version/status/scope or roll back, rather than assume any returned model proves persistence.

After commit, reuse `RefreshWorkforceEligibility` delivery and existing recovery attempts. Fingerprint equality coalesces unchanged operational state; it is not a durable command receipt. Out-of-order source/restriction revisions must be rejected or recorded superseded before writes; an old queued version cannot overwrite current evaluation. An H&S record closure/recovered status/RTW closure/CR response never revokes a restriction. A material source withdrawal/change requires an explicit review transition and refresh, not a silent declaration of fitness.

The existing observer cache, recheck completion and signal are distinct evidence. Persist each revision's impact outcome without adding a new queue: decision saved → delivery pending/processing/completed/failed/superseded; separately evaluation pending/assessed/partial/unavailable; separately review awaiting/accepted. Source saved or job queued does not mean duties evaluated, relief requested, cover accepted or publication safe. Evaluation exceptions preserve unavailable/prior successful evidence and retry metadata. A verified decision with an after-commit delivery failure remains saved with pending/failed delivery, not a fictional rollback.

Evaluate assignment, template/auto-schedule/publication through the same rule. Current/future assigned duties—including published work outside the near-term window—receive current-source rechecks and existing review signals. Active duty needs the actual replacement/relief workflow and a named lawful review owner; never infer the worker/assessor/old recorder is that owner. No auto-cancel, reassign, clock, pay, completed-duty rewrite, source-owned alert auto-resolution, publication acceptance or clinical-clearance mutation.
