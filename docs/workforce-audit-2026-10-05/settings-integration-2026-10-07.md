## Implemented scope

- A single permission-aware Workforce settings navigation entry, with the eMAR settings pattern: section navigation, searchable labelled rows, review dialogs, personal preferences, shared Hours & rest rules, history and links to their owning modules.
- Personal roster start-tab and initial layout preferences are used by the actual roster. Explicit links and in-session layout choices take priority. Per-user revision checks, audit and fresh approved-actor authorization are retained.
- Shared thresholds are read by current eligibility, roster capacity and reporting consumers. The existing Carbon3 receiver-order error in clipped scheduled hours is corrected; the baseline test still requires a real four-hour duty to report four hours.
- Typed save outcomes require an actual physical root commit and persisted values. Revision-only redirects do not certify success. Exact action, actor, prior/expected/result revisions, normalized values and changed/no-op state are matched. Global changes distinguish durable recheck staging from completion; no-op saves request no new recheck.
- Both forms retain drafts and the staffing reason after failed, stale, cancelled or unknown outcomes. Same-tick repeated requests and late callbacks are guarded. Unknown writes require explicit validated current-state review before another request. Reads retain the user's entries for comparison. Navigation warns before losing an uncertain result.
- Durable eligibility intents, worker progress, observations, source fingerprints, role pivot observation and minute recovery are connected. Queue/diagnostic/log failures after commit cannot turn a persisted policy/intent into a false failed-save claim.
- HR compliance-matrix removals now stage their eligibility recheck through the model observer in the same transaction; failed removals cannot leave a false success or a partial refresh.
- Freshness reads, roster projections and manual rechecks have their own exact approved-Site/worker/employee-duty scope. Status communicates pending, unavailable, stale or unverified checks, with no private source narrative. Retry acknowledgement means durable staging; unknown retries require a refreshed read. No automatic unassignment.
- New manual recheck writes use current locked authority and source tuple evidence, persisted intent verification and physical-root-only acknowledgement. Nested or otherwise unconfirmed outcomes are not labelled queued.
