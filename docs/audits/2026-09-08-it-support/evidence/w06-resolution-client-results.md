## Saved draft integration

The real shared `useItTicketDraft` and `TicketDraftRecovery` are integrated with the `public_resolution` purpose and canonical ticket context. The effective server feature flag controls usage. Runtime persistence remains disabled until the reviewed migration and explicit retention/configuration gate; this work does not choose or enable an operational policy.

Context checks read metadata only. Resume is explicit and validates the resolution payload, absence of attachments and the saved `base_ticket_version`; it never silently adopts `current_ticket_version`. A stale ticket needs canonical current-version review, explicit adoption and a new acknowledged save. Autosave is debounced800ms and saves the exact fields/base-version snapshot; empty untouched defaults are not presented as a saved resolution. Manual save, conflict review, explicit discard/start-new and session/access recovery reuse the canonical hook. Draft review content is withheld during canonical session recovery.

Submission requires an exact acknowledged snapshot and includes all three draft reference fields. A committed response must also acknowledge that exact UUID and submitted revision as consumed with revision+1. Missing or mismatched draft consumption leaves the business outcome unknown. The draft controls, autosave and edits remain suspended during an unknown canonical outcome. Confirmed resolution consumes only the submitted generation, not an arbitrary newer draft. Resolution accepts no staged files; public/internal reply consumption and scanner lifecycle remain W07 work.
