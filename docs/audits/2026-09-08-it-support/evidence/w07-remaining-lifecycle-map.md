## Operational decisions and external verification dependencies

DP07 at `implementation-plan.md:155` explicitly assigns scanner/retention/provider policy to operational owners. Outstanding decisions are separate from the implementable lifecycle gaps above:

1. **Attached-reply scanner outage behavior:** may the comment text commit while private file metadata remains withheld pending scan, or must the entire attached submission remain uncommitted with its exact draft/files retained? The plan does not select either. Do not silently choose what “reply submitted” includes.
2. **Quarantine and historical files:** approved retention/removal authority and policy for historical unverified objects. Do not invent an expiry or backfill a clean verdict from current metadata.
3. **Scanner operation and acceptance:** approved executable/service identity, process/storage permissions, signature freshness requirements and permitted real-engine test fixtures. Earlier read-only checks found no configured binary in the checked tool/user/machine environment and no scanner on that tool's PATH; this does not prove the state of an uninspected Herd process or another host. Real engine availability remains unverified.
4. **Persisted draft operations:** operational retention and key/backup ownership remain separate decisions; synthetic isolated-browser retention is verification-only. Registration and monitored execution of the existing pruner are still application work before activation.
5. **Provider acceptance:** authorized Microsoft/Gmail test accounts, support sender identity and test recipients are required for W11–W12 live acceptance. Isolated outbox/channel tests can continue meanwhile. No real communication or provider configuration is authorized by this review.
