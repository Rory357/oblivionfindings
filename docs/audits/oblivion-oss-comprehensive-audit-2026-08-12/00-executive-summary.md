## Retained findings

- **21 P0** — immediate safety, privacy/security or potentially unrecoverable integrity roots, including the source-confirmed cross-Site medication and Fleet booking reader/direct-object concealment failures and the browser-observed authenticated Shift task-provider failure retained against the audited commit.
- **67 P1** — serious authorization, continuity, finance, compliance or workflow controls. This includes the static Financial Insights direct-object guard/test gap, email-verification contract mismatch, compliance-export permission mismatch, inactive mixed-domain renewals-export boundary, the audited Clinical/Medication Lead account-creation blocker, destructible safeguarding evidence, and a generic payment-allocation writer that can bypass canonical journal-backed settlement. Historical remediation-register evidence records a merged creation-path change, but this audit did not re-establish its status on current `origin/main`; full end-to-end account creation and intended runtime role grants remain unverified.
- **12 P2** — material UI/accessibility/recovery improvements, including the browser-observed negative System Users count animation defect and My Day narrow-width StaffHeader overflow.

The five highest-leverage themes are:

1. One shared site/ownership/parent-child authorization graph for Control Room children, devices/custody/location, safeguarding, clinical, H&S, respite, fleet, HR and aggregated tasks.
2. Fail medication workflows closed: relationship-bound administrations, privileged structured overrides and current competency evidence.
3. End-to-end durable safety delivery: atomic source/outbox creation, stable signal identity, replay/dead-letter visibility and symmetric close/reopen transitions.
4. Govern legal/destructive authority: retention allowlists/dry-run/four-eyes/restore and consent/capacity/representative evidence that downstream services can safely consume.
5. Add provenance and exactly-once invariants to rostering, leave/payroll, funding claims and focused finance posting/reconciliation flows; suppress unsupported NZS/first-aid assurance, protect consent evidence from public storage, and replace the inconsistent public privacy statement with an owner-approved current NZ notice.

## Recommended first decisions

Immediately apply the stop-gaps in `findings.json` for P0s; do not use the medication override, quarantine retention execution, restrict global clinical/H&S/HR/device/location and Control Room child mutations, fail assignment when eligibility is unavailable, and reconcile safety sources without alerts. Suppress affirmative `Certified`/`Cover OK` claims until authoritative evidence exists, do not rely on public-disk consent files, and have the Privacy Officer validate export authority and the public notice. Then fund the shared authorization and durable-event foundations before module-by-module UI work.

This is a product/code gap assessment, not legal advice, clinical certification, penetration testing, financial assurance or regulatory attestation. Official NZ sources, source inference and specialist decisions are deliberately distinguished in the finding schema.
