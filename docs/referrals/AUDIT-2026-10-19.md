## P0 — must fix before any merge (affect everyone)

1. **Nobody can use Referrals after deploy.** The permission migration registers 32 keys and grants none; deploys skip seeders and `canDo()` has no admin bypass. The four independent keys (`funding.verify`, `proposals.approve`, `commence`, `settings.governance`) are held by no one.
2. **Existing people can no longer be activated or moved.** `Client::save()` refuses to create active people and runs every status/site/service change of an active person through `ReferralActivationGuard`, which requires a referral commencement no legacy person has. Site moves fail with "Failed to update client", onboarding completion no longer activates, reactivation is blocked, new clients default to onboarding and vanish from active-only lists.
3. **Respite check-in and extension blocked for every respite client** (`RespiteStayController.php:143,202`).

## P1

- Activation re-check breaks after routine care-plan/agreement edits (digest/fingerprint drift).
- Internal "Prepare support" cases can never be held or closed, and block a new preparation forever.
- Handover dead-ends on archived same-name people, >25 matches or matches the user can't open; no resolution path.
- "Ready for handover" stage unreachable.
- Eight demo/test seeders, the legacy `clients/create.tsx` page and ~7 tests create active clients and now throw.
- Retention: the policy fingerprint changed, so every approved retention policy is blocked until re-approved; referral data has no retention owner.
- Funding decisions: private pathway matched by literal id; external reference wrongly required; review attempts can link to open cases; submission forces acknowledgement text.

## P2

HR profile required even with `viewAllSites`; settings publish needs a second publisher and governance nobody holds; public intake abuse limits (per-IP only, notifies every assigner inside a row lock); SMTP inside a transaction; `today()` UTC for document effective dates; ~32 `canDo` calls per response; identity-write mutex row only seeded by migration; schedule-count test.

## Feature gaps (workflow)

Workspace search by reference only (names are encrypted — needs a blind index); missing saved views and filters; no Funding assistance or Follow-ups queues; staff phone intake can't be saved and resumed; staff can't send messages and there are no templates; no proposal → service agreement creation; settings lack archive/rollback/discard and configurable hold/close reasons and priorities; no funding body/host directory; reports lack small-group suppression, planned-vs-verified coverage and repeat-referral measures; no expiry/renewal follow-ups.

## Decisions for Stephan

1. **Activation policy.** Recommended: gate only referral-originated people, with an org switch "Require a service-commencement review before any person becomes active" (default off). Spec §18.22 asks for all routes to respect *applicable* gates; turning the switch on applies it organisation-wide.
2. **Public website replacement.** `/`, `/features`, `/pricing`, `/smart-monitoring` now point at the supported-living provider site; `/privacy-policy` and `/terms` show the provider's enquiry notice; `POST /contact` is retired. Confirm, and supply the real privacy policy text.
3. **Who holds the four independent keys** (funding verification, proposal approval, service start, settings governance).
4. **Virus scanning** on the test/production servers (`REFERRAL_EVIDENCE_SCANNER_*`): without it every upload is held in quarantine, which blocks funding verification and handover.
5. **Journey-branded public website** — see mockup v2.

### (B) Report privacy (small groups)

- **Config:** `config/referral-reports.php`, `small_group_threshold`, default 5. It can also be set with `REFERRAL_REPORTS_SMALL_GROUP_THRESHOLD`; 1 turns the rule off. It is not part of `ReferralSettings`.
- **Where it applies:** `ReferralReportPrivacy::protect()` runs at the end of `ReferralReports::build()`, so the page and the summary export get the same numbers. It covers:
  - headline measures;
  - received and contacted days in the trend;
  - mean contact delays;
  - every breakdown (stage, source, service, owner, hold and close reason, stage-age bands, region, classification);
  - the funding provider/income split, pathways, statuses, outcomes and pending-age bands;
  - working-time and history counts, means and totals.
- **Primary suppression:** a count from 1 to 4 becomes null with `suppressed: 'small'`, shown as "Fewer than 5". Zero stays 0. A hidden number is never sent as 0.
- **Secondary suppression:** in a breakdown, the next-smallest groups are hidden as well (`'paired'`, shown as "Hidden"). This continues until at least two groups are hidden, the hidden sum is at least 5, and the sum no longer pins a small group to one value. For example, 1 + 5 out of 36 also hides the 30. Hidden groups of 5 or more say "Hidden", never the untrue "Fewer than 5".
- **Regions:** hidden regions are combined into "Other reported regions", because a typed place name can itself identify someone.
- **Totals:** a total of 5 or more stays exact.
- **Choice:** the rule applies to everyone, whatever their access. §15 gives no exemption, only "suppress … where privacy policy requires". The matching-referrals list and the records export are unchanged: they list individual records under the same access as the referral pages.
- **UI:**
  - Hidden values read "Fewer than 5" or "Hidden", with a one-line `text-subtle` caption under the breakdown, chart or measures that hide something.
  - Hidden bars are not drawn and can't be clicked.
  - The trend line skips hidden days, with a note.
  - The handover gauge, the contacted donut and the handed-over bar show words instead of a share.
  - The source chart drops shares when a channel is hidden.
  - The export dialog says that small groups stay hidden.
  - Files changed: `report-charts.tsx`, `report-types.ts`, `report-history.tsx`, `report-working-time.tsx`, `reports.tsx`, `_report-dialogs.tsx`.
