## Required integration checks before enabling

Main runs these against the final integrated head; this map ran none.

1. **Route coverage/denial:** exercise each writer and every profile/API/correction alias above with flag off/on and unstarted/started stock. Each quantity route either uses the canonical pack path or rejects its obsolete shape. Forged person/order/stock/lot/count pairs, other unapproved sites and concealed CDs remain denied; no new held grants.
2. **Ordinary recorder:** FEFO across two packs; expired/quarantined packs excluded; exact stock-unit decimals and partial doses; explicit unsupported-unit rejection; scheduled/PRN/round/My Day/API/profile/transport calls all reach one allocator. Refused/withheld/missed/Self-managed records cause no ordinary dose allocation. Sufficient/insufficient tracked supply follows the approved recorded-dose policy without half-saved outcomes.
3. **Replay and concurrency:** same UUID after lost response yields one administration and one movement per allocated lot; changed payload/actor/target conflicts. Two doses/receipt/count/movement against the same stock serialize. P06 count-start and post-count-review revisions both reject stale packs; adding/removing a pack invalidates a pending correction.
4. **Rollback:** fail the second lot save, register/lot attachment, pharmacy total update, audit/event append or required follow-up. Administration, stock, lot revisions, CD dose/waste entries, count state, pharmacy totals and replay result roll back together. Notifications follow committed evidence; a returned “success” never hides missing allocation.
5. **Correction/reversal:** pending/rejected/note-only/approved replicas never allocate twice; CD forbidden edits still fail. Verified quantity compensation references original lots once, preserving later consumption. Double approval/reversal/retry/supersession cannot credit twice. Void destruction changes documentation only unless an explicit separate physical correction is provided.
6. **CD:** one witnessed receipt → one entry/one balance increment/one receipt-linked lot; replay attachment unchanged. Dose plus witnessed waste removes total once. Going out/coming back, count discrepancy, recount, found stock, loss, disposal and void/correcting entries keep physical pack total equal to register balance; expired physical CD stock is not erased. Count-origin loss has no second subtraction.
7. **Away and time boundaries:** outbound 10, dose used away 1, returned 9 reconciles once on both ordinary/CD paths. Cross NZ midnight without any write: last pack expires, displayed availability/meter/filter/safety/alerts agree immediately; printed expiry day remains usable. Mixed expired and fresh batches get independent warnings without false closure. Unknown balance/expiry and unprinted metadata remain distinct; no stock-unit conversion or historical data backfill is inferred.
