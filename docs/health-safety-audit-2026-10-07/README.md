# Health and Safety audit and specification

**7 October 2026 · Oblivion Findings · Proposed changes, not implemented**

The safety foundation is substantial: linked records, recoverable reporting, guarded closure and independent verification already exist. The experience obscures them with 21 sidebar items, oversized banners and long-lived modal workflows. Give every incident a real page, group navigation into six destinations and show the responsible person, next action and connected response/review clearly. Correct misleading status assurances, Control Room classification, time displays and permission mismatches alongside the layout changes.

**Selected direction:** a linked safety case workspace. Use the readability and ownership of a ticket page while preserving separate clinical, safeguarding, operational and H&S decisions. Control Room owns urgent response; the incident records the report; H&S owns safety review and corrective-action assurance. One named incident owner coordinates the work, with explicit reassignment and preserved history.

The user selected the responsible site manager/coordinator as the default recipient, with a monitored unassigned queue if unavailable. The completed plan contains 28 main work packages, 21 scope areas and 68 named acceptance scenarios, plus full register/integration parity. It includes market research, three review passes and the agreed cross-device reconstruction contract. Start with the detailed delivery plan below.

**Delivery requirement:** the user requires the full agreed implementation end to end, with Rory's current design rules throughout. Work packages are internal checkpoints, not permission to deliver partial scope. All relevant data, permissions, ownership, integrations, failure recovery and browser journeys must be verified before completion is claimed. No further broad product decisions are needed to begin development; concrete operational or provider dependencies must be resolved at their relevant gates.

The later Devices clarification makes this **one complete final release** of the agreed shared programme. The plans now share configurable internal/external response, versioned evidence windows and source access, and an HR-owned attendance recommendation with My Day confirmation/correction. Devices retains its broader equipment/endpoint implementation scope; common acceptance must be evidenced before the final release. See its [target scope](../security-devices-audit-2026-10-07/TARGET-SCOPE.md) and [Rory conformance mapping](../security-devices-audit-2026-10-07/RORY-CONFORMANCE.md).
