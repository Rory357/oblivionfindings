## Review paths

- Today: latest observed position, individual plan and next change; fictional map with selectable zones, zoom and enlargement; tracking source and independent access/sharing details.
- Zones & schedule: select weekdays and review canonical zone references. Add agreed zone walks through place/purpose, circle or polygon boundary, weekday/overnight/date schedule and exception, response proposal and review. Saving adds an inactive local draft. Existing geometry is not overwritten.
- Outings: destination and responsible worker search, explicit departure/arrival/return, travel context and draft review. No automatic alert exemption or extension.
- Scenario selector: outside active zones, missing arrival, unavailable tracker or uncertain boundary. Review alert demonstrates existing Control Room ownership, acknowledgement and an outcome recorded separately from observations.
- Activity: distinguish observations, future plans and worker responses, with type filters and governed export/Consents context.
- Review state: retained 20 privacy, authority, assignment, loading/failure and limited-permission examples. Collection, staff access, recipient sharing and self-access remain separate.
