## Most consequential remaining requirements

1. **Featured tiles must be an authored, movable block.** The core library and default document include a `featured` block, not a compulsory Home decoration (`company-hub-designer.html:871–874`). Current `resources/js/pages/company-hub/_layout.ts:3` and `app/Domain/CompanyHub/Services/HubContent.php:17` have no featured block type. `index.tsx` invokes `HubHero` ahead of the editable sections; `_hero.tsx:234` always produces its fixed collage. Editors cannot insert, move, duplicate, hide or remove this feature row, choose its lead independently, or use it on another page. The detailed contract and fallback behaviour are recorded below.

2. **Several real designer controls remain absent.** The source exposes per-block surface/tint, featured/news/link presentation, custom notice text, a call-to-action block, image caption and link description/page picker (`company-hub-designer.html:924–939,1039,1046`). Current `editor.tsx:1369–1604`, `_types.ts:39` and the `HubContent` block whitelist provide width, visibility, minimum height and most content inputs, but no block tone or presentation field, no custom notice/CTA block, and no image-caption or link-description input. Image caption rendering already exists at `_blocks.tsx:537`; link descriptions already render and are validated. These are missing authoring controls, not missing storage capabilities. `notices` is a canonical announcement feed and does not replace the editable source notice/callout.

3. **The designer lacks the page outline and illustrated block picker.** Source Settings/Page outline tabs, jump-to-section/block, hidden/width indicators, grouped preview cards, and corner resize are explicit at HTML `915,917,921,956,975–981,987,997`. Current `editor.tsx:623` has one settings inspector; `_designer.tsx:52,299` supplies sortable sections/blocks, a block-type dropdown and width slider. Existing drag, numeric height, width presets, move-to-section, undo/redo and reusable sections are functional. The outline/category picker and direct two-dimensional resize affordance are still missing. So are the explicit shortcut-help dialog and Ctrl/Cmd+Z / Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z handling (`HTML:964,997`); current toolbar undo works but no equivalent editor shortcut handler was found. The current canvas also forces every news block to one column and suppresses children after the second (`designer.css:600–604`), so it can differ from its published composition even when a wider block and larger limit are selected.

4. **Ideas need the approved ownership and progress journey.** `hub-community.js:65–72,116–120` includes a best-team selector when submitting, a named owner when reviewing, an explicit In progress stage, and a dated “What happened next” history retaining prior staff updates. Current `_community.tsx:36,44,268`, `HubCommunity.php:15,33,87` and `HubCommunityController.php:16,24` accept a category and overwrite one `status_note`; they expose no idea team/owner or staff progress history, and no In progress state. Submission, support, sorting, status response, discussion and removal exist. Category selection and an audit log are not substitutes for the missing routing/owner/history journey.

5. **Resource discovery is partial despite the richer guide reader.** `hub-library.js:15–28` and `hub-usability.js:5–7` specify browsing by team, searching title/topic/team/owner, collection tiles, saved/recent views and relevance ordering. Current `_resource-library.tsx:16–84` searches title/summary/category, filters categories/explicit collections, and provides cards/list, type, saved/recent and title/publication ordering. It has no team facet and does not search owner/team/topics. The source Process type (`hub-depth.js:99,116`) cannot be authored under `HubContent.php:100` or the current editor's resource type choices. The collection-photo row and left facet/help rail are also absent, which is a large composition difference; the specific fictional collection names/photos are not required production data.

6. **Knowledge follow-through omits the overdue warning and topic-related recommendations.** The contents rail, owner/current work contacts, review date, team help, personal checklist, helpful/report actions and version metadata are implemented and have recorded verification. However, `hub-library.js:45–50` adds a visible overdue-review warning and ranks related guides by shared topics plus team. `_knowledge.tsx:317` only prints the next review date; `HubKnowledge.php:28–36` selects related guides by team intersection alone. An unteamed guide with a shared topic therefore gets no topic-related suggestions.

7. **Newsroom/card controls are incomplete.** `hub-polish.js:18,52–53,81–86` supplies author-inclusive search, cards/list, author/date/read-time metadata, visible comment counts and direct save/unsave from each card. `_discovery.tsx:7` provides category/search/order but no list layout and searches only title/summary; `_blocks.tsx:41` has no author/read-time/comment footer or card bookmark action. Saving and conversation work after opening a story, which is a longer journey. The page “Show in company news” control at HTML `1038` also has no current contract/editor equivalent: every published `news` page is in the news feed. The source's quick Write a story action is currently reached through the general Create flow rather than a newsroom-specific entry point.

8. **Dynamic blocks are functional but their compact presentations differ substantially.** `hub-depth.js:89–90,108` has semantic service glyphs, a compact finder with faces/search/directory link, and team-scoped compact people/guides; `hub-polish.js:54` makes compact team cards photographic. Current `_dynamic-blocks.tsx:21` embeds the entire `PeopleDirectory`, and renders generic flat service/team links with one repeated icon. Team cover images and compact service semantics are lost. The finder can make Home very tall because it initially renders the full authorised directory; its Items to show control does not bound that presentation. Keep its full authorised search pool, but distinguish it from the compact on-page display. Source team filtering for news/knowledge blocks (`hub-depth.js:108,119`) is also unavailable on general/Home blocks, which currently offer category filters; department-page reader scoping covers only that context. Service team affiliation must come from a genuine canonical relationship if added, not a fabricated mapping.

9. **Home update discovery and navigation richness remain partial.** `hub-attention.js:48–52` always renders the Home pulse with new-since-last-visit and pending-confirmation count/CTA, including an all-caught-up state. Current `index.tsx:521–551,573` has the first announcement strip and Home actions but no equivalent pulse; update counts are accessible through My hub/Updates. `hub-refinement.js:7–17` adds icon/description discovery links and unread dots to More. `_navigation.tsx:76–185` correctly uses the shared measured rail and group menus, but presents label-only destinations with no description/unread treatment. The shared Rory rail itself is an established constraint and should stay stable.

10. **Draft recovery controls need an explicit equivalent.** The source allows leaving while discarding unsaved changes, restoring the starting design/page template with undo, and deleting only a saved unpublished draft while preserving its published page (`HTML:962,1068–1071,1077`). Draft deletion is blocked during review/scheduling until the workflow is withdrawn/cancelled (`hub-management.js:123`). Current manual save, history restore and archive are useful but do not offer these same choices: archive withdraws the whole page, and restoring history does not restore a selected starting template. Define safe equivalents around the existing persistence/review contracts; do not equate archive with draft deletion or silently discard a published revision.

## Featured block contract and reader fallback

These requirements are explicit in the source, rather than inferred from the fictional garden story:

- **Placement and lifecycle:** a normal section block (`HTML:871,873–874,889–890`) participates in add, move within/across sections, duplicate, remove, hide, width/minimum height and draft/history. It is usable wherever the block library is offered. The optional hero banner is a separate feature.
- **Presentations:** `mosaic` is a wider lead plus two stacked supporting tiles; `equal` changes the lead/support columns to equal width; `single` displays only the lead (`HTML:16,934,1045`). The source feature is 300px high by default and has no surrounding block heading/border/body padding. Existing app collage CSS is a fixed minimum 320px, with no author-selected variants (`hub.css:134–140`).
- **Lead selection:** the Featured story page picker uses published story records (`HTML:1039`). Changing it copies the selected title, summary and image into editable block fields (`HTML:1044`). The source block can then override heading, message and photo; media-library selection is available (`hub-management.js:25`). The reader links to the selected story and shows its category, author and read action (`HTML:1045`).
- **Source fallback:** `feature` chooses the requested story, otherwise the first available story; if there are none it shows “Publish a news story to feature it here.” It uses block title/message/photo where supplied and otherwise the chosen story's corresponding fields (`HTML:1045`). It does not invent a published story or replace the authored block with an unavoidable welcome collage. Current `_hero.tsx:259–338` instead shows a static welcome photo/title and two quick-link fallback tiles when news is absent; this is a different product behaviour and should be handled explicitly when retaining legacy Home compatibility.
- **Supporting stories:** use the first two different stories after excluding the lead; there are no separate supporting-story pickers in the preserved source (`HTML:1045`). Do not expand the approved requirements into unsupported arbitrary curation controls.
- **News deduplication:** the final override in `hub-polish.js:55` computes story IDs associated with visible featured blocks, excludes those from ordinary news when possible, and falls back to the category-matching visible story pool if exclusions would leave no news. It skips hidden featured blocks. The source's calculation also reserves two supporting IDs for the single variant despite CSS hiding them; that is a source quirk to resolve deliberately, not evidence that private/invisible reader content must be sent.
- **Production boundary:** resolve leads/supporting records from the viewer's currently authorised published pool. Withdrawn/private/expired records, retained copied metadata and source media need an explicit safe fallback. Preserve complete editor/history snapshots while removing hidden blocks and inaccessible references from reader/search/media access. Do not reproduce the mockup's use of static seed arrays as an authorisation rule.
