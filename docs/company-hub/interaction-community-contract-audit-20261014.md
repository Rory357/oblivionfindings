## Implemented and verified

“Verified” below means recorded backend test evidence for the stated behavior, not browser acceptance of every newly approved presentation.

- **1: Canonical recipients and sending.** Existing HR endpoints accept individual recipients or exactly one `to_department_id`, bind the real sender, and reject forged account/membership inputs. Multiple people create one kudos/post per recipient in one transaction. Whole-team recognition creates one typed kudos/post with a complete snapshot of current approved department members; empty, inactive, invisible and partially authorised teams are rejected. Preserve current staff, permissions, approved Sites and canonical audience boundaries. Sources: `app/Domain/Hr/Services/FeedService.php:86–140,305`, `HrTeamRecognition.php:34–80`, `routes/hr.php:1073`; evidence: `storage/logs/company-hub-team-recognition-agent-backend-current.log` (**39/351**, including nine team cases and existing HR regressions).
- **2: Reading, appreciation and shared spotlight.** Hub recognition projection requires public, current-authorised participants, current visible departments and the actual canonical feed audience. It returns typed person/team recipients, authorised work-profile links, reaction counts and `can_reply`. Replies remain limited to giver/recipient or currently eligible snapshotted team members; reading a public card does not grant reply rights. Shared spotlight selection/removal is persisted separately from personal reactions and requires Hub layout plus recognition permissions, exact actor and version. Sources: `HubRecognition.php:28–89,115–169`, `FeedService.php:37–79`, `HubRecognitionController.php:16`; evidence: `company-hub-recognition-agent-backend-final.log` (**7/163**) and HR **39/351** above. Actual queued team notification delivery is rechecked through both notifications' `shouldSend()` hooks; the team suite covers withdrawal, Site/department/audience change and deletion. No real delivery was performed.
- **3: Celebrations and birthday consent.** Birthday publication defaults off, is enabled per person through `company_hub.celebrations`, and never returns birth year, full DOB or age. Target management uses canonical current approved staff/Site scope. Work anniversaries and new starters derive from current staff records. Sources: `HubCelebrationController.php:16–35`, `FeedService.php:719–805`, `HubFeed.php:121,154`, `UserSiteAccessService.php:1356`; recorded evidence: the named birthday case in `company-hub-planning-backend.log` (**63/452** combined historical suite), with source test `HubPublishingTest.php:444`. This does not prove every new celebration UI or concurrent preference edit.
- **4: Page comments and moderation recovery.** Replies bind to a visible top-level comment on the same readable page. Ordinary readers receive no hidden body. Moderator hiding, inherited parent hiding, author removal and quarantined legacy hiding are distinct; restoring a parent never revives an independently hidden or author-removed child. Author-removed/legacy text is absent even from moderation review. Review/restore require current page access and moderation permission with actor, published revision and comment version; audit metadata excludes hidden text. Sources: `HubParticipation.php:28–78,82–99`, `HubCommentModeration.php:22–181`, `HubParticipationController.php:47–68`; evidence: `company-hub-moderation-agent-backend-final.log` (**12/134**).
- **8–9: Ideas.** Canonical optional team routing, named current visible owners, all six stages including `in_progress`, public dated append-only updates, idempotent support and discussion are persisted. Routing never narrows the existing public staff idea audience or reveals inaccessible work identities. Only moderators can publish progress; being the named owner grants no permission. Legacy surviving notes retain their known date without invented historical authorship. Actor/version conflicts deny persistence; non-Inertia JSON success is exactly `{saved:true,idea_id}` and Inertia still redirects. Sources: `HubIdeas.php:52–140,171–263`, `HubCommunityController.php:15–45`, `HubController.php:80`; evidence: `company-hub-ideas-agent-backend-current.log` (**12/279**) and `company-hub-ideas-agent-json-acknowledgement.log` (**1/18**, terminal exit 0).
- **10: Existing published polls.** Page authoring/publishing persists poll blocks. Current question/options/block ID determine the immutable vote key; one current vote per person is replaceable, changed questions start new results, stale keys conflict, hidden polls and unreadable pages deny direct writes. Sources: `HubContent.php:181–184,286`, `HubParticipation.php:15–43,120–138`; evidence: the named poll case in moderation **12/134**, plus `company-hub-designer-agent-backend-final.log` (**8/183**, hidden poll reads/direct writes).

## Implemented, but not verified against the complete approved journey

- Backend guards above do not establish the new preview/review layouts, success feedback, keyboard recovery, retained failed drafts or live staff acceptance. Root's `RESUME-HERE.md` explicitly records that populated Ideas progress history still needs browser verification.
- Poll closing exists only as a **date**, interpreted at the end of that NZ day. No recorded case here establishes the approved exact closing-time boundary, case-insensitive duplicate option denial, or results withholding before a person's vote.
- Birthday management is scoped, but `FeedService::getMilestones()` has no viewer argument and `HubFeed.php:121` passes its organisation-wide current-staff milestones through. Verify/document the intended company-wide opted-in celebration audience before claiming viewer-Site-scoped reading. This is a source boundary observation, not a reproduced unauthorised disclosure. Birthday updates have no actor/version conflict protocol.
- Common-state safety is endpoint-specific. Ideas, moderation and spotlight have recorded actor/version conflicts; polls use question keys. The mockup's generic two-column conflict comparison does not prove comparable server state for every action. Recognition sends are not idempotency-keyed, so replay after an uncertain response may create another record; preserving a draft is not proof of exactly-once sending.

## Missing contracts or explicit design differences

1. **Message-only recognition and optional personal detail are missing.** `recognitionRules():101` requires `category`; original `hr_kudos.category` is non-null (`2026_03_22_300001_create_hr_feed_and_kudos_tables.php:27`), send helpers require a string, and the shared wizard still gates category (`recognition-wizard.tsx:240–243`). The approved minimum is 10 trimmed message characters (`clarity.js:30–35`); current backend has only required/string/max:2000. Missing impact defaults to `good_job` (`FeedService.php:168,906`), whereas the approved untouched heading is `thank_you`. `HubRecognition::describe():155–169` also omits the already persisted impact. A coherent later change must support absent value through storage, validation, all HR callers/projections and value analytics; preserve old records; hide an absent value badge; and expose the saved impact. Do not silently substitute “Other” or rewrite historical impacts. Warm headings map to the existing four impact keys; arbitrary free-text headings are not required by this design.
2. **Recipient context and multi-person card semantics differ.** Hub's eligible picker currently exposes only `{id,name}` (`HubFeed.php:145`); approved search covers role, Site and team (`people.js:3`). Add only authorised public work metadata if needed. The prototype's one multi-name preview does not represent the current separate individual records. A real combined-person card needs an explicit canonical grouping contract, or review copy must accurately describe one card per person.
3. **Recognition reply removal and per-comment appreciation are absent.** The preview offers “Remove your reply” and individual comment likes (`people.js:9,13–14`). HR routes provide reply creation and permission-bound removal of whole kudos/posts, not author reply deletion (`routes/hr.php:1073–1090`). Page interactions support page reactions, not a reaction bound to a comment ID. Neither missing capability should be simulated as persisted. Use actual moderation provenance labels rather than the preview's illustrative “Checking context”; the approved design does not specify custom reason entry.
4. **Idea topic labels differ.** Approved topics are Working together, Staff wellbeing, Learning, Our spaces and Something else (`journey-finish.js:16`); canonical accepted categories remain Better ways of working, Our people, Community, Sustainability and Something else (`HubCommunity.php:12`). Decide a compatible mapping or additive migration before sending prototype labels. Backend minimum title/detail lengths also differ from the preview's 5/15; this audit does not change legacy records or rules.
5. **Poll exact times, immediate close/reopen and results policy are absent.** Approved input uses datetime/timezone (`community.js:12`); current schema accepts `Y-m-d` and `pollClosed()` uses end-of-day. There is no standalone live poll close/reopen endpoint; changes go through canonical page draft/review/publication. Participation JSON always includes counts/total, even before voting, so frontend hiding alone cannot enforce the approved results-after-vote contract. Trimmed, case-insensitive option uniqueness is also not the current authoring rule. Retain exact question-key and hidden-block protections when extending these contracts.
6. **Ideas/polls participation insights are missing.** Approved family 34 includes idea support and poll response summaries (`publishing.js:10`); `HubInsights` returns page engagement, health, events and searches only. Add aggregates and authorised links rather than raw supporter/voter identities. Current `readers` counts distinct recorded readers, not repeated visits, dwell time or understanding; do not relabel it as time spent. The coarse canonical topic taxonomy also differs from the fictional examples; draft-answer actions must use safe aggregate labels, not private search text.

Next backend slice should resolve optional recognition value/default heading/DTO first, then the explicit poll/results and participation-insights contracts. Keep canonical HR participation, per-person birthday opt-in, approved-Site work identities, public idea audience and moderation provenance intact. No completion or migration claim follows from the mockup's local state.
