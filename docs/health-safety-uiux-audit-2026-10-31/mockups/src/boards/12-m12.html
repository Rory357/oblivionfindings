Asking to close anyway, and recording the WorkSafe decision

  • "Ask to close anyway" (720) uses the server's rules: one request covers one open item, and closing needs every such item covered, so it is offered only when exactly one is left. The pop-up names that item. A reason of at least 20 characters; an evidence reference; the date the request lapses and a review date on or before it (each the end of that day, NZ time; the lapse date can be up to 29 days ahead, because the server refuses anything past 30 days from asking).
  • The owner, or someone who can close any review, can ask; with no owner, as here, anyone at the site who may ask can. The person who asks can't approve it. Approvers' names come from the server.
  • "Record the WorkSafe decision" (480) opens only after someone has taken the review on (the server refuses it before). The legal meaning stays word for word in its hint; the example reason uses the Act's own test.
  • "Yes" leads straight to "Record that WorkSafe was told" (M8).
