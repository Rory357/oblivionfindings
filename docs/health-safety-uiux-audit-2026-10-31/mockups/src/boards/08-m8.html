The pop-ups: take it on, add an action, tell WorkSafe, close

  • All on the pop-up kit: round lens, title, one line, ✕, the horizon line, the record it came from, a grey footer with Cancel first and the main button named for what it does.
  • Owners come from a searchable picker that lists only people allowed to own it, and says so. An empty picker says why and who to ask.
  • Dates use the approved calendar with the chosen date shown; times use the approved clock with manual entry, AM/PM and the time zone.
  • "Record that WorkSafe was told" has exactly the route's fields: when, how, reference, and whether the site is being kept as it was until WorkSafe releases it. The decision pop-up is on M12.
  • "Close" asks what we learned (outcome note) and says it can't be undone (there is no reopen). "Close anyway" is offered only when exactly one item it can cover is still open (M12).
  • Typing then Esc, ✕ or Cancel asks "Discard this draft?" (kit rule; not drawn).
